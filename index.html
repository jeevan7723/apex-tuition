Tuition & Admission FAQs

Everything you need to know about the admission procedure, fee payment, batch adjustments, and classroom policies.

We provide total financial flexibility for parents:

  • Monthly Payment Plan: Simple recurring fee payable by the 5th of every month.
  • Quarterly Plan: 3-month cycle with an automatic 8% fee discount.
  • Annual Comprehensive Package: Lump-sum with up to 20% discount + free study kit.
  • 0% Interest EMI: Spread full-year fees across 6 or 9 easy interest-free bank installments.

Testing and performance benchmarking are at the heart of our methodology:

  • Weekly Chapter Tests: Held every Saturday covering topics taught in the preceding 5 days.
  • Bi-Weekly Unit Cumulative Tests: Cross-chapter tests evaluating long-term retention.
  • Full-Length Board Simulation Mocks: 15 real-exam timed papers with identical marking schemes and personalized feedback on step-marking and presentation.
  • Online Analytics Portal: Parents receive instant SMS test scores and graphical learning-gap breakdowns.
